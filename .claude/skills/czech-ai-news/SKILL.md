# Czech News

- Run the folliwg script that fetches the latest news summaries from Hospodarske Noviny.

```bash
bash {baseDir}/scripts/fetch-news.sh
```

- DO NOT CHANGE OR TRANSLATE THE CONTENT OF THE NEWS SUMMARIES.
# - Quickly scan the news summaries and group them by topic (e.g., business, technology, economy).
- Show the grouped news summaries to the user.
